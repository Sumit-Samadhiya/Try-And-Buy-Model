# 🛍️ Doordrape Shopkeeper Partnership & Ground Onboarding Playbook
*Asset-Light Pilot Edition (0-Inventory Risk)*

---

## 🎯 Objective
Local boutique aur clothing store owners ke saath **Zero-Inventory-Risk** model par partner karna. 
- Dukandar ka stock unke paas hi rehta hai.
- Hum sirf top 10-15 trending pieces photo shoot karke app par list karte hain.
- Order aane par rider 30-min doorstep trial karata hai.
- Bik gaya toh instant UPI payment, nahi bika toh 60 minute ke andar original condition mein wapas.

---

## 1. ⏱️ Ground Pitch Script (Under 2 Minutes)
*Tone: Respectful, confident, business-friendly, non-tech jargon.*

> **Opening (Icebreaker):**  
> "Bhaiya/Uncle ji namaste! Aapka collection kaafi trendy aur fresh hai, main pichle 2 din se dekh raha tha. Bas 2 minute ka ek solid business proposal tha aapke liye, jisme aapka **₹0 kharcha** hai aur daily extra sale banegi."

> **The Core Pitch:**  
> "Main **Doordrape** chala raha hoon. Aaj kal online apps (Myntra/Ajio) pe logon ko size aur fitting ki badi problem aati hai. Humare area ke customers chahte hain ki wo kapda pehle apne ghar ke aaine me try karein, fir khareedein.  
> 
> Hum aapki dukaan ke **top 10-15 trending designs** apne platform par dikhayenge.
> - Aapka kapda aapki dukaan par hi rahega.
> - Jaise hi hamare app par kisi customer ka 'Doorstep Trial' order aayega, hamara rider aayega aur wo specific piece le jayega.
> - Customer ke ghar 20-30 minute mein trial hoga.
> - **Agar customer ne rakh liya:** Aapka poora selling price (MRP/Agreed rate) usi waqt aapke UPI pe transfer ho jayega.
> - **Agar size pasand nahi aaya:** 60 minute ke andar rider wo kapda safe garment bag mein aapki shelf par wapas rakh dega."

> **Closing (Low-Friction Hook):**  
> "Aapko koi advance maal nahi dena, koi advance security nahi deni. Sirf 7 din ka ek chhota sa pilot karte hain 5-10 pieces ke saath. Agar aapki 10-15 extra bikri ho gayi toh aapka fayda, nahi toh aapka kapda toh aapki dukaan par hi hai. Aaj 10 best pieces ka photo le lein?"

## 2. 🛡️ Objection Handling Guide

### Q1: "Kapda ganda ya phat gaya toh? Customer ne kharab kar diya toh?"
> **Aapka Answer:**  
> "Bhaiya, bilkul sahi aur wajib sawal hai. Iske liye hamari **3-layer safety policy** hai:
> 1. **Dukaan se nikalte waqt:** Rider aapke saamne 10 second ka video aur photo lega ki kapda intact hai, tag laga hai aur koi daag nahi hai.
> 2. **Security & Trial Rule:** Customer ko rider tabhi kapda deta hai jab wo footwear utaar kar indoor try kare. Koi makeup ya perfume spray allow nahi hota.
> 3. **100% Damage Guarantee (Written):** Agar 1% bhi daag, silai khulna ya damage hua — **Doordrape wo kapda usi waqt aapse poore daam mein khareed lega.** Aapko ₹1 ka nuksan nahi jhelna padega."

---

### Q2: "Mera offline grahak aa gaya aur kapda trial par hua toh?"
> **Aapka Answer:**  
> "Bhaiya, do baatein hain:
> 1. **Sirf 60 Minute Window:** Hamara rider order aane par item le jata hai aur trial 20 minute ka hota hai. Total cycle time sirf 45–60 minute ka hota hai.
> 2. **WhatsApp Ping / Alternative Size:** Jaise hi rider kapda uthayega, aapke register par entry hogi. Agar by chance usi 45 minute mein koi walk-in customer wahi exact piece maang leta hai, aap hume bas ek WhatsApp message daal dena — agar customer ne finalize nahi kiya hoga toh rider turant wapas le aayega ya customer ko agla piece dikha denge.
> 3. **Stock Buffer:** Hum wahi designs list karte hain jisme aapke paas 2-3 piece ya 2 sizes available hon."

---

### Q3: "Tumhe kya milega? Commission kitna loge?"
> **Aapka Answer:**  
> "Bhaiya, pilot run (pehle 50 orders) mein hamara target profit kamana nahi, aapke saath relation banana aur customers ko service dikhana hai.
> - **Option A (Markup):** Agar aapka suit/shirt ₹800 ka hai, toh hum app par ₹899–₹950 dikhayenge convenience fee jodkar. Aapko aapke pure ₹800 milenge.
> - **Option B (Small Margin):** Agar MRP ₹1200 hai aur aap ₹900 me bechte hain, toh aap hume ₹850 lagaiye, hum ₹950 pe sell karenge.
> - **Zero Deduction:** Delivery ya packaging ka ₹1 bhi aapke bill se nahi katega."

## 4. ⚙️ Operational Best Practices for the First 50 Orders

1. **Tag-Seals (Tamper-Proof Tagging):**
   - Dukaan ke tag ke saath Doordrape ka ek reusable colored zip-tie / cable tag lagayein (`"Do Not Remove for Trial"`). Agar tag toot gaya, toh customer ko khareedna hi padega.
2. **Dedicated Garment Carrier Bag:**
   - Rider ke paas premium dust-proof zipper garment bag hona chahiye taaki bike par kapde par dhool ya crease na aaye.
3. **Daily 10:00 AM WhatsApp Sync:**
   - Subah 10 baje shopkeeper ko 1 minute ka WhatsApp message: *"Bhaiya, listed 12 items stock mein hain na?"* Taki out-of-stock items app par pause ho sakein.
4. **Instant UPI Screenshot:**
   - Jaise hi customer UPI kare, rider usi second dukandar ke phone par UPI screenshot bhejega ya soundbox bajega. Payment transparency builds 100% loyalty.
5. **Start with 2km Radius Only:**
   - Shuruat mein shop se sirf 2-3 km doori wale delivery pin-codes kholo taaki 30-45 minute mein round-trip complete ho sake.
