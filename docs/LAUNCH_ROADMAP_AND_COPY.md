> **First Completed Doorstep Trial with $\ge 1$ item kept/purchased.**

* To get **500 active purchasing users**:
  * Expected keep rate per trial: **70%** (industry benchmark for try-before-you-buy is 65–75%).
  * Doorstep trials needed: $\frac{500}{0.70} \approx \mathbf{715\text{ trials}}$.
  * Booking-to-delivery fulfillment rate: **85%** $\rightarrow \mathbf{840\text{ trial orders}}$.

### 📍 Week 1: Hyper-Local Pincode Infiltration (Target: 75 Users)
*Focus on 1–2 dense delivery zones (e.g., Vijay Nagar / Palasia / Scheme 54) to keep delivery times under 30 minutes.*

1. **High-Rise Apartment & Society WhatsApp Gateways (The Resident Angle)**:
   - Identify 5 largest gated societies in your active service zone.
   - Message society secretary or active resident admins offering an **exclusive society perk**: *"Exclusive Doorstep Trial Day: Try dresses at home before paying. Special 15% society coupon code (e.g. `SILVER54`)."*
   - Frictionless: Post a clean 1-image flyer + 3-line invite with direct WhatsApp ordering link.
2. **Local Boutique & College Hostels Guerilla Outreach**:
   - Girls' hostels and PG accommodations are hotbeds for weekend party shopping.
   - Put up a physical QR poster on hostel notice boards: *"Date night tonight? Try 3 dresses in your room in 30 mins. ₹0 advance."*

### 🔄 Week 4: The "Mirror Selfie" Viral Loop (Target: 150+ Users $\rightarrow$ Cumulative: 500+)
*Turn every successful trial into 2 new trials automatically.*

1. **In-Trial Packaging Gamification (The Mirror Tag)**:
   - Print a small card in every trial kit:
     > *"Loved your look? Snap a mirror selfie, tag @Doordrape on your IG Story, and get ₹200 OFF instantly on your purchase!"*
   - Immediate social proof broadcast to all of their friends who live in the same city.
2. **WhatsApp "Shopping Buddy" Pass**:
   - When a trial completes successfully, auto-send a WhatsApp message:
     > *"Hey [Name], your trial was a success! 🌟 Give your bestie a VIP Doorstep Trial pass. Send them this link: [invite-link]. When they keep their first outfit, you both get ₹150 store credits."*
   - Viral coefficient target ($K$): 0.35 (every 3 customers bring in 1 new customer for free).
