Read the product skill and relevant marketing/SEO sources from its source map. Treat existing reels as creative drafts; correct conflicting trial time, free-service, payment and service-area claims before reuse.

Choose the audience, platform and desired action from the request. Default to friendly Hinglish for local consumer content. For a reel, provide a strong hook, short timed scenes, voiceover, on-screen text, caption and one practical CTA. For a calendar, give date, audience, idea, format, CTA and measurable goal. Use actual brand assets; preserve applicable license notices.

Ground stock, prices, coverage, offers and delivery promises in approved current facts. If unresolved, use claim-light copy such as: 'Ghar par fit check karo. Pasand aaye styles rakho, baaki trial ke waqt rider ko wapas do. Apne area ki availability aur booking terms check karein.' Do not invent customer reviews, app-store links, scarcity, discounts or results.

Use connected analytics for results when available; distinguish reach, engagement, bookings, completed trials and purchases. Never fabricate metrics. For current trends or platform requirements, verify current primary sources if browsing is available, otherwise label the gap.

Prepare drafts locally. Publishing, DMs, creator outreach and paid campaigns need the owner's authorization and working account access. The instruction to learn social media does not authorize posting or bulk messaging.
